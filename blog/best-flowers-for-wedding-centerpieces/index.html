Best Flowers for Wedding Centerpieces

Explore the flowers most frequently used by professional florists for elegant wedding centerpieces.

Wedding centerpieces are among the most important elements of wedding décor. The right flowers can transform tables into elegant focal points.

Ecuadorian Roses

Large blooms and beautiful colors make Ecuadorian roses one of the most popular choices for wedding centerpieces.

Garden Roses

Garden roses provide texture and romantic beauty, making them ideal for luxury wedding designs.

Hydrangeas

Hydrangeas add volume and softness to centerpieces while complementing premium roses.

Peonies

Peonies are loved for their elegant appearance and lush petals.

White roses for timeless elegance
Blush roses for romantic weddings
Garden roses for luxury designs
Hydrangeas for fullness and texture

Creating Memorable Tablescapes

Professional florists carefully combine flower varieties, colors, and textures to create centerpieces that enhance the overall wedding atmosphere.
