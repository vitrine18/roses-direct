Documents We Manage

Every shipment includes the necessary paperwork required for international flower trade.

Phytosanitary Certificate

Issued by Ecuadorian authorities to certify plant health and compliance with U.S. import standards.

Commercial Invoice

Detailed invoice including product description, quantities, origin, and declared value.

Air Waybill (AWB)

DHL Express shipment document with full tracking and routing details.

Packing List

Box-level breakdown of stems, varieties, and weights for customs clearance.

Customs Declarations

Export and import declarations aligned with U.S. customs requirements.

USDA / APHIS Compliance

Documentation aligned with USDA and APHIS regulations for fresh flowers.
