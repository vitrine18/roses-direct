Our goal is simple: solve problems quickly and fairly.

Every shipment of Ecuadorian roses is prepared with care, but fresh flowers travel through an international supply chain involving farms, export handling, customs, airlines, and final-mile delivery.

If your order arrives with a problem, please contact us promptly. The sooner we receive the information, the easier it is to understand the condition of the shipment and determine what happened.

01

First, document everything.

When your roses arrive, we recommend checking the shipment before putting the flowers into production.

01

Upon Delivery - Check the outer box

Look for visible crushing, punctures, moisture damage, broken seals, or other signs that the package may have been mishandled during transit.

02

Document the Condition

Take clear photographs of the outer packaging, shipping label, box condition, and any affected flowers. If possible, photograph the issue before opening or altering the shipment.

03

Contact Us Within 24 Hours

Claims related to quality, condition, or visible shipping damage must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.

04

Keep Everything

Please do not discard damaged packaging or affected flowers until we have had an opportunity to review the case.

Please do not discard damaged packaging or affected flowers until we have had an opportunity to review the case.

03

Quality concerns.

Because roses are a natural and perishable product, their condition can change after delivery depending on hydration, temperature, storage, handling, and vase care.

For this reason, a quality concern should be reported as soon as possible after delivery, preferably within 24 hours. Photographs taken when the shipment arrives are especially helpful.

REPORT

On arrival

Damage, missing quantities, severe quality issues, or visible problems with the shipment.

DOCUMENT

Before use

Photograph the roses and packaging before the flowers are processed or used in an event.

04

When the issue involves shipping.

Farm Direct Roses coordinates the shipment from Ecuador, while DHL Express handles the international transportation and delivery portion of the journey.

If a shipment appears to have been damaged in transit, we may need to work with the carrier to investigate the circumstances. This is why photographs of the box, packaging, and contents are important.

CARRIER REVIEW

Carrier-related claims may be subject to DHL Express terms and conditions, applicable transportation rules, and any declared-value or shipment-protection coverage associated with the shipment.

05

A few important limits.

We will always make a reasonable effort to help investigate legitimate problems with an order. However, some circumstances may be outside Farm Direct Roses' direct control.

01

Delays, customs decisions, inspections, weather events, airline disruptions, or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

02

Changes in flower condition that occur after delivery due to improper hydration, storage, temperature, handling, or vase care.

03

Carrier liability and claim procedures are governed by the applicable DHL Express terms and conditions and any mandatory law or international convention.

04

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Farm Direct Roses' liability is limited to the value of the affected product to the extent permitted by applicable law.
