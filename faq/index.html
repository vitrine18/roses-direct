Do you deliver directly to my business?

Yes. Our farm-direct model is designed for door-to-door delivery using DHL Express. Shipments can be delivered to a business address in the United States, subject to carrier, customs, and destination requirements.

Why buy Ecuadorian roses?

Ecuador is known for rose production at high altitude, with equatorial sunlight and growing conditions suited to premium fresh-cut roses. Depending on the variety, Ecuadorian roses can offer long stems, large blooms, strong stems, rich colors, and good vase performance.

How does farm-direct sourcing work?

We coordinate sourcing directly with selected Ecuadorian farms. Roses are harvested, graded, prepared, and packed at origin before being shipped internationally. This farm-direct approach helps keep the path between origin and customer clear and organized.

How long does delivery take?

Timing depends on rose availability, farm preparation, flight schedules, customs processing, and destination. As a planning guideline, allow approximately 10–14 business days from payment confirmation to delivery. We recommend allowing additional time for important events and peak floral seasons.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Order quantities depend on the product, rose variety, farm availability, and destination. For first-time buyers, we can discuss manageable starting volumes so you can evaluate the process without committing to a large ongoing program.

Can I request specific varieties or stem lengths?

Yes. Tell us the rose varieties, colors, stem lengths, quantities, and timing you need. We check current farm availability and can recommend suitable alternatives when a requested variety or specification is not available.

How are the roses kept fresh during shipping?

Roses are harvested and prepared at origin before being packed for international transport. Proper hydration, grading, packaging, and careful handling are important parts of maintaining flower quality throughout the journey.

Can I order for Valentine's Day or Mother's Day?

Yes. Peak floral seasons require additional planning because demand, farm availability, and transportation capacity can change. We recommend contacting us as early as possible to discuss your varieties, quantities, and desired delivery date.

Do you offer payment terms or credit?

Orders are currently handled on a prepaid basis. Payment confirmation allows us to secure the requested roses and begin the preparation and export process.
