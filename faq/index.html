Do you deliver directly to my business?

Yes. Our farm-direct model is designed for door-to-door delivery using DHL Express. Your shipment can be delivered directly to your business address in the United States, subject to the carrier and destination requirements.

Why buy Ecuadorian roses?

Ecuador's high-altitude growing conditions, equatorial sunlight, and volcanic soil are well suited to premium rose production. Depending on the variety, Ecuadorian roses are known for strong stems, large blooms, rich colors, and excellent vase performance.

Discover the Farm Direct difference ↗
How does farm-direct sourcing work?

We coordinate sourcing directly with selected Ecuadorian farms. Roses are harvested, graded, prepared, and packed at origin before being shipped internationally. This helps reduce unnecessary handling and storage between the farm and the customer.

How long does delivery take?

Timing depends on availability, farm preparation, flight schedules, customs processing, and destination. As a planning guideline, allow approximately 7–14 business days from payment confirmation to delivery. We recommend planning additional time for important events and peak seasons.

View the delivery timeline ↗
Is there a minimum order quantity?

Order quantities depend on the product, farm availability, and destination. For first-time buyers, we can discuss manageable starting volumes so you can evaluate the process without committing to a large program immediately.

Can I request specific varieties or stem lengths?

Yes. Tell us the varieties, colors, stem lengths, quantities, and timing you need. We will check current farm availability and recommend suitable options when a requested variety or specification is not available.

How are the roses kept fresh during shipping?

Roses are harvested and prepared at origin before being packed for international transport. Proper hydration, grading, packaging, and controlled handling are important parts of maintaining quality throughout the journey.

Can I order for Valentine's Day or Mother's Day?

Yes. Peak seasons require additional planning because demand, farm availability, and transportation capacity can change. We recommend contacting us as early as possible so we can discuss your varieties, quantities, and delivery date.

Do you offer payment terms or credit?

Orders are currently handled on a prepaid basis. Payment confirmation allows us to secure the requested roses and begin the preparation and export process.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

We review your requirements and respond with availability, recommendations, pricing, and logistics information based on your destination and order. If you are a first-time buyer, we can also walk you through the process before you commit to an order.
