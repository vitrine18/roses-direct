THE FARM DIRECT PROCESS

One conversation. Four simple steps.

You don't need to manage the farms, coordinate international shipping, or figure out the process on your own. We coordinate each stage and keep you informed along the way.

YOUR ORDER JOURNEY

From Farm to Your Door.

01
START WITH A CONVERSATION

Tell Us What You Need

Share the varieties, stem lengths, quantities, timing, and destination you're considering. If you're not sure what to order, we'll help you determine what makes sense for your project or business.

02
SOURCING & QUOTATION

We Build Your Quote

We check current farm availability and prepare a quotation based on your requirements. Your quote can include the roses, stem specifications, and international shipping through DHL Express.

03
CONFIRMATION & PREPARATION

Your Roses Are Secured

Once your order is confirmed, we coordinate with the farm to secure the requested roses and organize the preparation required for international shipment.

04
HARVEST & DELIVERY

From Ecuador to Your Door

Roses are harvested, prepared, packed, and shipped through DHL Express with tracking. Your shipment continues directly toward its destination, with communication throughout the journey.
