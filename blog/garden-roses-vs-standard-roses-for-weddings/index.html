Garden Roses vs Standard Roses: Which Is Better for Weddings?

One of the most common questions wedding florists ask is whether to use garden roses or standard roses for wedding designs.

Both options offer unique advantages, and the right choice often depends on the style, budget, and vision of the event.

01
THE ROMANTIC CHOICE

What Are Garden Roses?

Garden roses are known for their layered petals, romantic appearance, and luxurious texture. They are often chosen for high-end weddings and premium floral installations.

01

02
THE CLASSIC CHOICE

What Are Standard Roses?

Standard roses feature a more classic rose shape and are extremely versatile for floral work. They are available in a wide range of colors and stem lengths.

TWO DISTINCT STYLES

Garden roses bring depth, texture, and a romantic garden-inspired character. Standard roses offer versatility, consistency, and a classic silhouette.

03
BEST FOR

When to Choose Garden Roses

  • Luxury wedding bouquets
  • Premium floral installations
  • Romantic wedding themes
  • High-end event designs
04
BEST FOR

When to Choose Standard Roses

  • Large wedding projects
  • Centerpieces
  • Corporate events
  • Everyday floral arrangements
05
THE DESIGN APPROACH

Can Florists Use Both?

Absolutely. Many floral designers combine garden roses and standard roses to create depth, texture, and visual interest in wedding designs.

06
THE FINAL CHOICE

Choosing the Right Rose for Your Event

Both garden roses and standard roses can create stunning wedding flowers. The best choice depends on the desired aesthetic, flower availability, and overall event style.
