Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sourcing premium Ecuadorian roses directly from the farm.

We work with professional buyers who value clarity, reliability, and transparency. Below you’ll find answers to the most common questions we receive from new clients.

Who do you work with?

+

We work exclusively with professional buyers such as florists, wholesalers, event designers, floral studios, we also open for first time buyers. Our model is designed for businesses that require consistency, premium quality, and dependable logistics. Whether you are an experienced importer or sourcing directly from Ecuador for the first time, we’re here to help.

I’ve never imported flowers before. Can you help me?

+

Absolutely. Many of our clients are first-time buyers. We guide you step by step—from selecting varieties and stem lengths to logistics and delivery—so the process is simple and transparent. give us a try!

How do orders work?

+

Once we understand your needs, we confirm availability, pricing, and logistics. Orders are prepared directly at the farm and shipped fresh to your destination. Let's beging!

Do you ship directly to my door?

+

Yes. We work with trusted DHL logistics partners to deliver your flowers to your door (USA Only). Easy delivery to your door! Other markets to your local airport.

What makes your roses different?

+

Our roses are sourced directly from high-altitude Ecuadorian farms and shipped without intermediaries or long-term storage. This results in longer stems, stronger heads, and extended vase life.

How does the farm-direct shipping work?

+

Roses are harvested, hydrated, packed at origin, and shipped via DHL Express directly to your door. This minimizes transit time and preserves freshness at every step.

How long does delivery take?

+

Typical delivery times range from 3 to 4 business days from farm to destination address, depending on location and customs processing. We coordinate closely to ensure reliable arrival windows.

What minimum order quantities do you require?

+

Minimums could be small as a QB (100-125 stems depending on varieties). Our goal is to remain flexible while maintaining logistical efficiency. Contact us to discuss quantities that fit your business needs.

Do you offer tinted or specialty roses?

+

Yes. We work with select farms that specialize in tinted roses, garden roses, and premium varieties. Availability depends on season and advance planning.

How do you ensure freshness upon arrival?

+

All flowers are shipped directly from the farm to preserve freshness, vase life, and overall quality. Our freshness strategy includes immediate post-harvest hydration, temperature-controlled packing, and fast air transport.

Are prices stable during peak seasons?

+

Peak seasons such as Valentine’s Day and Mother’s Day experience natural market fluctuations. Early planning helps secure better availability and pricing.

Do you offer credit terms?

+

No. All orders are prepaid prior to shipment. This allows us to secure fresh inventory, work directly with farms, and maintain consistent quality and reliability.

Why is prepaid required?

+

Flowers are a perishable product. Prepaid orders ensure transparency, faster processing, and reduce risk for all parties involved.
