How to Keep Roses Fresh Longer

Simple techniques to maximize vase life and preserve the beauty of premium Ecuadorian roses.

Fresh roses can remain beautiful for many days when properly cared for. A few simple practices can help maximize freshness, hydration, and vase life.

01
THE FOUNDATION

Use Clean Water

Fresh, clean water helps roses absorb moisture efficiently and reduces the presence of bacteria that can interfere with hydration.

SIMPLE PRACTICE

Keep the vase clean and replace the water regularly to give your roses the best possible environment.

02
THE STEM

Trim the Stems

Cutting the stems at an angle can improve water absorption and help roses remain properly hydrated after they are placed in a vase.

01
WATER

Change water regularly to maintain a cleaner environment for the flowers.

02
LIGHT

Keep roses away from direct sunlight and excessive heat.

03
LEAVES

Remove damaged leaves that sit below the water line.

04
ENVIRONMENT

Store flowers in a cool environment whenever possible.

03
THE ENVIRONMENT

Temperature Matters

Cool temperatures help roses maintain freshness and can slow the natural aging process of the flower.

°C
KEEP IT COOL

Avoid placing roses near direct sunlight, heating vents, appliances, or other sources of excessive heat.

04
THE BEGINNING

Start with Premium Roses

The quality and freshness of roses at the beginning significantly influence vase life and flower performance.

Beautiful roses begin with freshness — and continue with thoughtful care.
