OUR STORY

Our Story & Philosophy

Farm Direct Roses connects professional floral buyers with premium Ecuadorian roses through a more direct, transparent sourcing approach.

From Ecuadorian rose farms to the people creating beautiful work with them.

WHY WE STARTED

We created Farm Direct Roses to make sourcing Ecuadorian roses more direct.

Our goal is to give florists, designers, wedding professionals, and other floral buyers a clearer way to source roses from Ecuadorian farms, with communication and logistics coordinated around each project.

From Ecuador, directly to your design.
THE DIFFERENCE

Why We Exist

Buying flowers through multiple layers of distribution can make sourcing less transparent and add complexity between the farm and the final buyer.

Our approach is centered on direct sourcing from trusted Ecuadorian farms and coordinating the preparation and international movement of each order around the buyer's needs.

Fewer layers.
Greater transparency.
More direct sourcing.
