ORDERING QUESTIONS

Before You Place Your Order.

Do I need to know the exact rose variety before contacting you?

No. You can tell us the color, style, event, arrangement, or overall look you are trying to create. If you have a specific variety in mind, include it and we can check current availability.

What information do you need to prepare a quotation?

We generally need the rose variety or type, quantity, preferred stem length, desired timing, and USA delivery destination. If some details are still undecided, send us what you know and we can clarify the remaining information.

Do you coordinate international shipping?

Yes. International transportation is coordinated through DHL Express, and the quotation can include the shipping component for the destination you provide.

Can first-time importers order Ecuadorian roses?

Yes. Our process is designed to make farm-direct sourcing easier to understand for professionals and first-time importers in the USA. You can start with your requirements and questions, and we can explain the next steps.
