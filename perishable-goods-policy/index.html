Perishable Goods Policy

Fresh-cut roses require a different kind of care from the moment they leave the farm to the moment they reach your hands.

BEFORE YOUR ORDER ARRIVES

Fresh flowersare not ordinary goods.

Ecuadorian roses are fresh-cut agricultural products. Their quality depends on harvesting, preparation, transportation, temperature, hydration, and handling throughout the journey.

Because flowers are perishable by nature, a few important differences apply to floral orders that do not apply to conventional merchandise.

THE DETAILS

What to know about your shipment.

These guidelines help protect the quality of your roses and make the receiving process as smooth as possible.

01

Fresh-Cut & Perishable

Roses are harvested specifically for an order and prepared for international transport. Once cut, they naturally begin to change over time, which is why timing and proper care matter throughout the supply chain.

Unlike non-perishable merchandise, flowers cannot be stored indefinitely or returned for resale after delivery.

02

Final Sale

Due to the perishable nature of fresh-cut flowers, all sales are final once the shipment has departed from origin. Returns are not accepted.

This policy does not prevent a customer from submitting a valid claim when roses arrive damaged or when there is a documented issue with the shipment.

03

Inspect Your Roses Promptly

Please inspect your shipment as soon as possible after delivery. Check the outer packaging first, then examine the roses for visible damage, quality concerns, or other issues.

If something does not look right, document the condition with clear photographs and contact us promptly.

Review our Claims Policy →
04

Care After Delivery

Once the shipment has been delivered, the buyer is responsible for providing appropriate care to the roses. This includes prompt unpacking, hydration, clean water, proper preparation, and suitable storage conditions.

The sooner flowers are properly processed after arrival, the better positioned they are to perform throughout an event or floral production schedule.

05

Storage & Handling

Roses should be kept in an appropriate cool environment and protected from excessive heat, direct sunlight, dehydration, freezing conditions, and other factors that can accelerate deterioration.

Proper storage and handling after delivery are essential to maintaining flower quality and vase performance.

06

Delivery Delays & External Conditions

International flower shipments can be affected by circumstances outside our direct control, including customs processing, airline schedules, weather, transportation disruptions, or other carrier-related events.

When a delay or external event occurs, we will work with the customer and available logistics partners to understand the situation and determine the appropriate next step.

07

When Something Goes Wrong

We understand that fresh flowers are often purchased for important weddings, events, and professional floral work. If there is a genuine issue with your shipment, please contact us as soon as possible.

Our Claims Policy explains what information we need in order to review a reported issue and determine the appropriate response.
