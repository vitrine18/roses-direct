PAYMENT DETAILS

Prepaid Orders Why Prepaid? Payment Methods Questions

At Farm Direct Roses, we believe payment should be straightforward from the beginning.

Because roses are perishable and each order requires coordination with the farm, preparation, and international shipping, our orders are handled on a prepaid basis.

Prepaid Orders Only

All orders must be fully prepaid prior to shipment. We do not currently offer credit terms or payment upon delivery.

Every order of fresh roses requires planning before it leaves the farm. Prepayment helps us coordinate that process efficiently and prepare the shipment according to your confirmed requirements.
