YOUR INFORMATION, RESPECTED

Privacy Policy

A straightforward look at how we handle the information you share with Farm Direct Roses.

A NOTE FROM US

Your information should be handled with care.

When you contact Farm Direct Roses, request a quotation, or inquire about an order, you may share information about your business, your destination, and the flowers you are looking for.

We respect that information. We use it to understand your needs, communicate with you, prepare quotations, and coordinate the services you request — not to sell your information to someone else.

THE DETAILS

What you should know

We believe privacy information should be understandable, especially when you are trusting a business with your details.

01

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us or using our website.

Depending on the purpose of your inquiry, this may include your name, company name, email address, phone number, business information, shipping destination, requested rose varieties, quantities, stem lengths, delivery timing, and other details needed to prepare your inquiry or order.

02

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used to respond to your questions, understand your requirements, prepare quotations, confirm availability, and communicate with you about an order or potential order.

We may also use information necessary to coordinate sourcing, preparation, shipping, delivery, and other services you have specifically requested.

03

Information Shared for Shipping

International flower shipments require certain information to move from the farm to the final destination.

When necessary to fulfill an order, relevant information may be shared with logistics providers, shipping carriers, customs authorities, or other parties involved in the transportation and clearance of the shipment.

We only share information that is reasonably necessary for the service being provided or where disclosure is required by law.

04

We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal or business information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Your information is used primarily to communicate with you and provide the products, quotations, logistics, and support you request from Farm Direct Roses.

05

Communication With You

If you submit an inquiry, we may contact you by email, phone, WhatsApp, or another communication method you have provided in connection with your request.

These communications may include quotations, availability, order details, shipping information, documentation requests, delivery updates, or responses to questions you have asked.

06

Website Information

Like many websites, our site may use standard technologies that help us understand how visitors use the website and improve its performance and usability.

We do not use the information submitted through our forms as a means of selling or distributing customer contact information to unrelated businesses.

07

Protecting Your Information

We take reasonable care when handling the information entrusted to us and limit access to information according to the needs of the business and the service being provided.

However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08

How Long We Keep Information

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to communicate with you, provide requested services, maintain business records, resolve inquiries, or meet applicable legal and accounting requirements.

09

Your Choices

You may contact us if you have questions about information you have provided to Farm Direct Roses or if you would like to request clarification regarding how that information is being used.

We will make reasonable efforts to respond to legitimate requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

10

Changes to This Policy

As our website, services, or legal requirements evolve, we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date so that visitors can see when the policy was last reviewed.

A SIMPLE PROMISE

Your information helps us serve you. It should never work against you.

Whether you are requesting your first quotation or coordinating an upcoming shipment, we believe communication should be clear, purposeful, and respectful of the information you provide.

QUESTIONS?

We're happy to clarify.

If you have a question about the information you provide through our website or during the ordering process, please contact us.
