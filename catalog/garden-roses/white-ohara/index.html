White O'Hara

Premium White Garden Rose

White O’Hara is the ultimate expression of purity and sophistication. Its creamy ivory petals open into a large, full garden-style bloom with exceptional texture, depth and form.

Highly favored for bridal bouquets and high-end wedding installations, this variety brings elegance without the hardness of a stark white. Its soft ivory tone creates a refined, romantic presence in both intimate arrangements and larger floral installations.
