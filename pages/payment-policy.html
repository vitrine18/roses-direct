Payment Policy

Clear, transparent payment terms for all wholesale orders.

At Farm Direct Roses, we focus on transparency, reliability, and quality. To ensure a smooth process
and protect the integrity of every shipment, the following payment policy applies to all wholesale orders.

Prepaid Orders Only

All orders must be fully prepaid prior to shipment. We do not offer credit terms or payment upon delivery.

Why Prepaid?

  • Flowers are perishable and require precise planning
  • Prepayment secures inventory directly at the farm
  • Ensures faster processing and on-time delivery
  • Reduces risk and delays for all parties

Accepted Payment Methods

  • • PayPal (credit & debit cards accepted)
  • • Bank transfer (available for approved wholesale clients)

All orders are prepaid.

Questions?

If you have questions regarding payments, logistics, or your first order, our team is happy to assist you
and guide you through the process.
