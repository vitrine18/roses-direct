01 The Classic Rose

A timeless flower, grown with purpose.

Standard roses remain one of the most versatile flowers in professional floral design. From weddings and editorial installations to retail bouquets and everyday arrangements, their recognizable silhouette makes them a foundation of the floral industry.

Farm Direct connects you with Ecuadorian-grown roses through a direct sourcing model designed around freshness, consistency, and professional-quality stems.
