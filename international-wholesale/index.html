FROM ECUADOR TO THE WORLD

A proven flower-export origin.

Ecuadorian flowers are exported to markets around the world, with roses among the country's best-known floral products. International buyers source Ecuadorian roses across North America, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

Our role is to help you access that supply through a direct sourcing conversation — understanding your requirements, identifying suitable roses, preparing the order for export, and coordinating the appropriate logistics for your destination.

DESTINATION
