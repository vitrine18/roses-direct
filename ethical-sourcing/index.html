Our Ethical Principles

Every partner farm is evaluated beyond quality — ethical responsibility is non-negotiable.

Fair Labor Practices

Safe working conditions, legal wages, social security, and respect for labor rights across all operations.

Worker Well-Being

Access to healthcare, training, and long-term employment stability within farming communities.

Environmental Responsibility

Water management, reduced chemical use, and soil protection practices.

Certifications & Compliance

Many of our partner farms follow internationally recognized standards such as FlorEcuador, Rainforest Alliance, and other responsible farming frameworks.

Compliance is verified through audits, inspections, and long-term relationships built on transparency.
