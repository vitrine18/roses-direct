Why Farm Direct Changes Everything

A smarter, fresher, and more transparent way to source premium Ecuadorian roses — without middlemen, warehouses, or unnecessary delays.

What “Farm Direct” Really Means

Farm Direct is not a marketing term. It is a supply model designed to remove friction, preserve freshness, and give professionals direct access to Ecuador’s best rose farms.

No Middlemen

You work directly with farms, not brokers or resellers. This means transparent sourcing, better pricing, and full product traceability.

Maximum Freshness

Roses are shipped immediately after harvest. No storage, no repacking, no warehouse delays — just fresher blooms that last longer.

Simplified Logistics

DHL Express handles the entire international delivery process, ensuring fast, secure, and door-to-door shipping.

Designed for Florists & Professionals

Our Farm Direct model is built specifically for florists, event designers, professional and first time buyers who demand consistency, quality, and reliability.

You do not need to be an importer or have prior experience with international shipping. We simplify the process so you can focus on your business, not paperwork.

  • • No minimum importer requirements
  • • Clear pricing and transparent processes
  • • Ideal for weddings, events, and high-volume needs
  • • Direct communication and personalized support
