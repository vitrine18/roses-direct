INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING

Customs & Documentation Made Clearer.

International flower shipments involve export documentation, transportation requirements and customs procedures. We help keep the process clear by coordinating the shipment details from origin and communicating the information needed for delivery.

Export documentation is prepared at origin, while DHL Express provides shipment tracking and handles the transportation and applicable customs processes associated with the shipment.
