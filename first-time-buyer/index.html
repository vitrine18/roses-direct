FIRST-TIME BUYER QUESTIONS

A Few Questions You May Already Have.

I've never ordered flowers internationally. Can I still order?

Yes. You do not need previous experience with international flower sourcing to start a conversation with us. We guide you through the information needed for your order and explain the process along the way.

What if I don't know which rose varieties I need?

That is completely fine. Tell us what you are designing, the colors or style you have in mind, and any other requirements you know. We can help you understand the available options.

How does shipping from Ecuador work?

Once your order is prepared, the shipment is coordinated for international delivery through DHL Express. We provide guidance throughout the process so you know what is happening at each stage.

Do I have to place an order immediately?

No. You can start by asking questions and understanding your options. The first conversation is simply a way to determine whether Farm Direct is the right sourcing solution for your needs.
