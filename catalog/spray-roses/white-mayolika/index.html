White Mayolika

Premium White Spray Roses

White Mayolika brings a luminous, pure-white character to floral design with the natural fullness of a multi-bloom spray rose. Its clean appearance and delicate clustered blooms make it one of the most versatile choices for elegant floral work.

The crisp white tone works beautifully with soft neutrals, greenery, blush tones, and pastel palettes, allowing designers to create arrangements that feel refined, airy, and timeless.

Pure White Multi-Bloom 40–70 cm Farm Direct
A Versatile White Palette

Designed for timeless floral work.

White Mayolika is especially valued when a design requires softness, purity, and visual fullness without the formality of a traditional single-bloom rose. Its clustered structure creates natural volume and texture, making it an excellent companion flower for larger focal roses, garden-style flowers, greenery, and delicate seasonal accents.

From intimate bridal bouquets to larger wedding installations, retail mixed bunches, and sympathy work, White Mayolika gives florists a dependable white spray rose that can move easily between classic and contemporary styles.
