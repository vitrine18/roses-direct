Clear terms for ordering and receiving fresh Ecuadorian roses from Farm Direct Roses.

BEFORE YOU ORDER

A clear understanding comes first.

We believe buying roses directly from Ecuador should feel straightforward. These terms explain the general conditions that apply when ordering from Farm Direct Roses.

Because fresh-cut flowers are perishable and international shipments may involve carriers, customs authorities, farms, and other service providers, certain aspects of an order may also be subject to the applicable terms and requirements of those parties.

WHO WE WORK WITH

Professional floral businesses first-time importers.

Farm Direct Roses primarily works with professional florists, wedding and event designers, floral studios, and other businesses sourcing Ecuadorian roses for professional use. We also work with first-time importers who are preparing their first international rose order.

ORDERS & AVAILABILITY

Every order begins with confirmation.

An inquiry or quotation request does not constitute a confirmed order. Variety, stem length, quantity, timing, destination, and availability must be reviewed and confirmed before an order is finalized.

Availability may vary according to farm production, season, demand, and other agricultural conditions. A quotation therefore confirms the specifications and availability applicable to that particular order.

PRICING & PAYMENT

Your order is confirmed once payment is secured.

Pricing is confirmed according to the specifications and availability of each order. Prices may vary depending on variety, stem length, quantity, season, destination, and shipping requirements.

Orders are currently handled on a prepaid basis. For more information, please see our Payment Policy → .

SHIPPING & DELIVERY

From Ecuador to your door.

Farm Direct coordinates the preparation and international shipment of confirmed orders. Depending on the order and destination, delivery may involve farms, carriers, customs authorities, DHL Express, and other service providers.

Transit times and delivery conditions may therefore be affected by circumstances outside our direct control, including customs processing, transportation schedules, weather, and carrier operations.

International shipping arrangements and applicable delivery charges are confirmed as part of the individual quotation.

PERISHABLE FLOWERS

Roses require care from the moment they arrive.

Fresh-cut roses are agricultural products and are naturally perishable. Proper handling, hydration, temperature, and storage after delivery are important to maintaining flower quality.

Please review our Perishable Goods Policy → for additional guidance on receiving and caring for fresh-cut roses.

CLAIMS & SHIPMENT ISSUES

If something doesn't look right, let us know promptly.

If your shipment arrives with a quality or condition concern, please inspect the flowers as soon as possible and document the condition of the shipment with photographs and any relevant information.

Our Claims & Liability → page explains the information and steps that may be required when reporting a shipment issue.

PRODUCT INFORMATION

Nature has a little variation of its own.

Rose color, bloom size, stem length, availability, and appearance may naturally vary according to variety, season, growing conditions, and farm production.

Product specifications and availability are confirmed as part of the individual quotation and order process.

A NOTE FROM FARM DIRECT

We would rather make the process clear than complicated.

If you have questions about an order, availability, payment, shipping, or what to expect when your roses arrive, please contact us before placing your order. We are happy to help you understand the process.
