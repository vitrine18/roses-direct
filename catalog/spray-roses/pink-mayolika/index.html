THE SPRAY ROSE FORM

Small blooms. Soft expression.

Spray roses bring a different kind of beauty to floral design. Instead of one large focal bloom, each stem carries multiple flowers that create rhythm, texture and natural volume.

Pink Mayolika brings a delicate soft pink presence to this expressive form, adding romance, texture and natural movement to bouquets, arrangements and sophisticated event work.
