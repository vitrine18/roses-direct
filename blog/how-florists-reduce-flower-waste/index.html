THE CHALLENGE

Flower waste is more than a product issue. For florists and event professionals, damaged stems, reduced freshness, and short vase life can directly affect the quality of the final design and the experience delivered to clients.

Fresh flowers are one of the most important investments in a floral business. When flowers arrive in better condition and with more usable life ahead of them, florists have greater flexibility to create, design, and serve their clients with confidence.

Why Flower
Waste Happens

Flowers can pass through multiple stages before reaching a florist. Every additional stage of storage, handling, and transportation can affect the condition of the product.

The longer flowers remain in the supply chain, the less usable life may remain when they finally reach the designer or studio.
