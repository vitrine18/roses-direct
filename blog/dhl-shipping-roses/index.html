DHL Express Shipping from Ecuador Explained

Shipping fresh roses internationally requires careful coordination to maintain quality and freshness throughout the process.

Our farm direct model is designed to simplify the experience for florists and event professionals in the USA while we manage sourcing, logistics, and delivery preparation.

1. Order Review

Orders are carefully reviewed and payment verification is completed before sourcing begins.

2. Farm Sourcing

Premium roses are sourced directly from Ecuadorian farms based on availability, freshness, and flower quality.

3. Export Preparation

Flowers are prepared, packed, and organized for international shipment under export conditions.

4. DHL Express Delivery

Orders are shipped directly to the customer's door in the USA using DHL Express logistics and tracking support.

Estimated Timeline

Because flowers are freshly sourced and prepared specifically for each order, the complete process may typically take approximately 8 business days from order processing to final delivery.

This timeline helps ensure fresher roses, improved flower quality, and careful international handling throughout the shipping process.

A Simplified Experience for Florists

Our goal is to simplify international flower sourcing by handling the logistics, sourcing coordination, and delivery process so florists can focus on their floral designs and clients.
