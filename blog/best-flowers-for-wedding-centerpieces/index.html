Best Flowers for Wedding Centerpieces

Explore the flowers most frequently used by professional florists to create elegant, memorable wedding centerpieces.

A wedding centerpiece does more than decorate a table. It helps establish the atmosphere of the celebration, bringing together color, texture, scale, and floral beauty.

01
THE FOUNDATION

Ecuadorian Roses

Large blooms and beautiful colors make Ecuadorian roses one of the most popular choices for wedding centerpieces. Their elegant form allows florists to create arrangements that feel refined without overwhelming the table.

01
SIGNATURE BLOOM

Large, elegant blooms create immediate visual impact in luxury wedding tablescapes.

02
ROMANTIC TEXTURE

Garden Roses

Garden roses provide texture and romantic beauty, making them especially suited to luxury wedding designs where layered petals and a softer floral appearance are desired.

ROMANTIC • TEXTURED • LUXURIOUS

Their layered petals add depth and softness to sophisticated wedding arrangements.

03
VOLUME & SOFTNESS

Hydrangeas

Hydrangeas add volume and softness to centerpieces while complementing premium roses. Their generous shape can help create fuller arrangements without relying entirely on large quantities of focal flowers.

THE BALANCE

Volume creates the canvas. Roses create the focal point.

04
LUXURY BLOOM

Peonies

Peonies are loved for their elegant appearance and lush petals. When available and appropriate for the season, they can bring an unmistakably luxurious quality to wedding tablescapes.

05
THE PALETTE

Choosing the Right Floral Style

The most successful centerpieces usually combine flowers, colors, and textures intentionally. The right combination depends on the wedding style, venue, season, and overall visual direction of the event.

01

Timeless

White roses for a refined and classic aesthetic.

02

Romantic

Blush roses for soft and romantic celebrations.

03

Luxurious

Garden roses for layered, premium designs.

04

Full & Textured

Hydrangeas for volume, softness, and dimension.

THE TABLESCAPE

Beautiful tables begin with thoughtful floral composition — not simply more flowers.

06
THE EXPERIENCE

Creating Memorable Tablescapes

Professional florists carefully combine flower varieties, colors, and textures to create centerpieces that enhance the overall wedding atmosphere.
