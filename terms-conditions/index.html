01

BEFORE YOU ORDER

A clear understanding comes first.

We believe buying roses directly from Ecuador should feel straightforward. These terms explain the general conditions that apply when working with Farm Direct Roses.

Because fresh flowers are perishable and international shipments may involve farms, carriers, customs authorities, and other third parties, certain aspects of an order may also be subject to the applicable terms of those parties.

02

WHO WE WORK WITH

Professional floral businesses

Farm Direct Roses primarily works with professional florists, wedding and event designers, floral studios, and other businesses sourcing roses for professional use.

03

ORDERS & AVAILABILITY

Every order begins with confirmation.

An inquiry or quotation request does not constitute a confirmed order. Variety, stem length, quantity, timing, and availability must be reviewed and confirmed before an order is finalized.

Availability may vary according to farm production, season, demand, and other agricultural conditions.

04

PRICING & PAYMENT

Your order is confirmed once payment is secured.

Pricing is confirmed according to the specifications and availability of each order. Prices may vary depending on variety, stem length, quantity, season, destination, and shipping requirements.

Orders are currently handled on a prepaid basis. For more information, please see our Payment Policy → .

05

SHIPPING & DELIVERY

From Ecuador to your door.

Farm Direct coordinates the preparation and international shipment of orders. Delivery may involve farms, cold rooms, airlines, customs authorities, DHL Express, and other logistics partners.

Transit times and delivery conditions may therefore be affected by circumstances outside our direct control, including customs processing, transportation schedules, weather, and carrier operations.

06

PERISHABLE FLOWERS

Roses require care from the moment they arrive.

Fresh-cut roses are agricultural products and are naturally perishable. Proper handling, hydration, temperature, and storage after delivery are important to maintaining flower quality.

Please review our Perishable Goods Policy → for additional guidance.

07

CLAIMS & SHIPMENT ISSUES

If something doesn't look right, let us know promptly.

If your shipment arrives with a quality or condition concern, please inspect the flowers as soon as possible and document the condition of the shipment.

Our Claims & Liability → page explains the information that may be needed when reporting a shipment issue.

08

PRODUCT INFORMATION

Nature has a little variation of its own.

Rose color, bloom size, stem length, availability, and appearance may naturally vary according to variety, season, growing conditions, and farm production.

Product information and availability are confirmed as part of the individual quotation and order process.

A NOTE FROM FARM DIRECT

We would rather make the process clear than complicated.

If you have questions about an order, availability, payment, shipping, or what to expect when your roses arrive, please contact us before placing your order. We are happy to help you understand the process.
