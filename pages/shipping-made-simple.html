Shipping & Customs Made Simple

Importing fresh flowers does not need to be complicated. Our DHL Express shipping model is designed to minimize paperwork and simplify the process for buyers.

Door‑to‑Door DHL Express Delivery

Orders ship directly from Ecuador and are delivered to your business address in the United States, typically within 2–3 days.

Customs Clearance Support

DHL handles the customs clearance process. Buyers may be required to provide basic business information, but no complex import setup is necessary for most shipments.

Clear Cost Visibility

Any applicable duties, taxes, or fees are communicated clearly. There are no surprise warehouse or handling charges after arrival.

Ideal for First‑Time Buyers

Many of our clients place their first international flower order with us. Our team guides you through each step to ensure a smooth experience.
