The Silver Effect

A rose with a metallic point of view.

Silver changes the way a rose interacts with its surroundings. Instead of relying on a traditional floral color, the metallic finish introduces light, reflection, and cool tonal contrast into the arrangement.

The result is a rose that feels architectural and contemporary, particularly when paired with whites, neutrals, deep greens, glass, chrome, or modern event materials.
