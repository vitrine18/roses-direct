Standard Rose Collection
The Classic Rose

A timeless flower, grown for professional design.

Standard roses are the classic single-bloom rose used across professional floral design. Their recognizable form, broad color range, and choice of stem lengths make them a versatile foundation for bridal bouquets, event installations, retail arrangements, centerpieces, and everyday studio work.

Our Ecuadorian standard roses are sourced with professional use in mind. Farm Direct connects florists and floral businesses with roses grown in Ecuador's high-altitude growing regions, with availability confirmed according to variety, color, stem length, and harvest.

The Ecuadorian Advantage

Why Ecuadorian roses stand apart.

Ecuador's high-altitude growing environment is one of the reasons the country's roses are sought after by professional floral buyers. For designers, the difference is expressed in the stem, the bloom, the color, and the overall presence of the flower.

01

Strong, Long Stems

High-altitude growing conditions contribute to the long, sturdy stems valued in professional floral design, especially for bouquets, installations, and large-scale arrangements.

02

Generous Blooms

Standard Ecuadorian roses are known for their distinctive bloom presence, giving arrangements a strong focal point while maintaining the familiar elegance of the classic rose.

03

Rich Color Selection

From classic reds and whites to pinks, peaches, yellows, lavender tones, and bicolor varieties, standard roses offer a flexible palette for weddings, events, retail, and editorial work.

Designed for the Floral Trade

One classic form, many ways to use it.

Standard roses remain a dependable choice when a design calls for recognizable rose form, visual structure, and a wide range of colors. They can carry an arrangement as the primary flower or work alongside garden roses, spray roses, foliage, and other seasonal flowers.
