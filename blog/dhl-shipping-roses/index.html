How fresh Ecuadorian roses move from farm sourcing to your studio, shop, or event doorstep in the USA.

Farm Direct Journal Shipping Guide

Shipping fresh roses internationally requires more than simply placing flowers in a box. Every order needs careful coordination between sourcing, preparation, export handling, and international delivery.

At Farm Direct, our model is designed to make that process easier for florists, designers, wedding professionals, and first-time buyers who want access to premium Ecuadorian roses without navigating the entire sourcing process alone.

01
THE JOURNEY

From Ecuadorian Farms
to Your Door

Every Farm Direct order follows a coordinated process. Roses are sourced according to the order requirements, prepared for international transportation, and then shipped directly to the customer's destination in the USA.

The goal is simple: keep the journey organized while protecting the quality and freshness of the flowers along the way.

01

Order Review

Each order is carefully reviewed before sourcing begins. Quantity, varieties, stem lengths, destination, and delivery requirements are confirmed so the sourcing process can begin with clear specifications.

02

Farm Sourcing

Roses are sourced directly from Ecuadorian farms according to availability, freshness, quality, and the specific requirements of the order.

03

Export Preparation

Once sourced, the flowers are prepared and packed for international transportation, with attention to handling and export requirements before the shipment leaves Ecuador.

04

DHL Express Delivery

The prepared order is shipped internationally through DHL Express, providing transportation, shipment tracking, and delivery support to the customer's destination in the USA.
