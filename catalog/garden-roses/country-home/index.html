Country Home

Premium Salmon Garden Roses

Country Home is a romantic garden rose defined by its warm salmon tone and beautifully layered rosette form. Each bloom opens with depth and movement, creating the lush, naturally gathered appearance that makes garden roses so distinctive.

Its soft warmth works beautifully with ivory, champagne, blush, peach, and natural foliage. The result is an elevated palette that feels sophisticated without becoming overly formal.

A particularly beautiful choice when the flower itself needs to become part of the visual story.
