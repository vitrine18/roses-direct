WHAT SUPPORTS THE SHIPMENT

The documents behind every shipment.

Depending on the shipment, destination, and applicable requirements, several documents may support the international movement of fresh Ecuadorian roses.

01

Phytosanitary Documentation

Documentation related to the phytosanitary condition of the shipment and applicable agricultural requirements for exporting fresh flowers from Ecuador.

02

Commercial Invoice

Provides the commercial details of the shipment, including the products, quantities, country of origin, and declared value used for customs processing.

03

Packing List

Identifies the contents of the shipment and provides useful box-level information for transportation, handling, and customs review.

04

Air Waybill

The transportation document associated with the shipment, providing routing, shipment, and tracking information during international delivery.

05

Customs Information

Shipment information is prepared and transmitted as part of the customs process, helping the carrier and relevant parties process the shipment.

06

Agricultural Requirements

Fresh flowers are subject to agricultural requirements designed to help protect plant health when shipments enter the destination market.
