The Light Blue Effect

A rose designed
to soften the light.

Light Blue creates a completely different visual language from traditional rose colors. Its soft pastel tone reflects light gently across the petals, creating an airy appearance that feels contemporary without becoming overly bright.

In large installations, the color works beautifully as a quiet foundation. Paired with whites, creams, blush tones, or complementary pastel flowers, it creates arrangements that feel refined, fresh, and intentionally understated.
