Logistics, Simplified

We remove the complexity of importing flowers. From Ecuadorian farms to your door in the USA — fast, controlled, and transparent.

Importing roses does not need warehouses, intermediaries, or complicated logistics. Our farm-direct model, combined with DHL Express, ensures premium flowers arrive exactly as they were harvested — fresh, hydrated, and ready to sell.

How It Works

Farm-Level Harvest & Preparation

Roses are harvested at peak maturity and immediately processed at origin. Stems are hydrated, graded, and packed directly at the farm — not in third-party warehouses.

Controlled Packing & Cold Chain

Each shipment is packed using cold-chain standards designed for long-distance air transport, preserving stem strength, bloom integrity, and vase life.

DHL Express Air Transport

Shipments are flown directly from Ecuador to the United States via DHL Express, minimizing transit time and reducing handling risks.

Door-to-Door Delivery

Flowers arrive directly at your business address. No consolidation warehouses, no secondary distributors, no unnecessary delays.

Customs & Documentation

Many buyers hesitate because they believe importing flowers requires complex paperwork. Our logistics process is designed to be accessible, even for first-time buyers.

Export documentation is prepared at origin, and DHL provides customs clearance support in the United States, guiding each shipment through the process.

No Import Expertise Required

You do not need to be a licensed importer or have prior experience. We work with florists, event designers, buyers of all sizes, first-time buyers providing guidance at every step of the shipment.
