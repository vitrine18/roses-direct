WHAT SUPPORTS THE SHIPMENT

The documents behind every shipment.

Depending on the shipment and destination, several documents may be involved in moving fresh Ecuadorian roses internationally.

01

Phytosanitary Documentation

Documentation issued by the appropriate Ecuadorian agricultural authority to certify the phytosanitary condition of the shipment when required.

02

Commercial Invoice

Provides the commercial details of the shipment, including the products, quantities, origin, and declared value required for customs processing.

03

Packing List

Identifies the contents of the shipment and provides useful box-level information for handling and customs review.

04

Air Waybill

The transportation document associated with the shipment, providing routing and tracking information throughout the delivery process.

05

Customs Information

Shipment information is prepared and transmitted as part of the customs process, helping the carrier and relevant authorities process the shipment.

06

Agricultural Compliance

Fresh flowers are subject to agricultural requirements that help protect plant health and maintain compliance when entering the destination market.
