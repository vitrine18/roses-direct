THE TIMING MATTERS

Fresh flowers require careful coordination.

Fresh-cut roses are time-sensitive by nature. Their journey does not begin when the box leaves Ecuador — it begins when your order is confirmed and the requested roses are secured.

We coordinate sourcing, preparation, export documentation, international transportation, and final delivery as one connected process.

The timeline below is a planning guide, not a guaranteed delivery date. Actual timing may vary depending on variety availability, customs processing, airline schedules, destination, and other circumstances outside our control.

BEYOND THE CLOCK

What can affect delivery timing?

International flower shipments involve several moving parts. Understanding them helps you plan with realistic expectations.

01

Variety Availability

Some varieties and stem lengths may require additional coordination with partner farms.

02

Seasonal Demand

Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day, weddings, and major events can create significantly higher demand for premium roses.

03

Customs Processing

International shipments may require additional processing at the destination before final delivery.

04

Destination & Transport

Transit time can vary depending on destination, flight schedules, weather, and carrier operations.
