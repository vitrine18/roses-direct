Why Long Stem Roses Matter for Professional Floral Design

In professional floral design, every detail matters. One of the most important factors florists consider when selecting roses is stem length.

Long stem Ecuadorian roses are highly valued because they offer greater flexibility, elegance, and visual impact in premium floral arrangements.

The Visual Impact of Long Stem Roses

Long stems naturally create a more luxurious appearance. They add height, movement, and sophistication to floral designs while giving florists more creative freedom.

Creates more dramatic floral arrangements
Provides greater design flexibility
Ideal for premium wedding installations
Enhances luxury floral presentations

Popular Stem Lengths for Professional Florists

Professional florists often prefer roses ranging from 60 cm to 100 cm depending on the project requirements.

  • 60 cm – Everyday floral work
  • 70 cm – Wedding bouquets and centerpieces
  • 80 cm – Luxury arrangements
  • 90–100 cm – Large floral installations and premium events

Why Ecuadorian Roses Excel

Ecuador's growing conditions allow roses to develop strong stems and large blooms, making them ideal for high-end floral applications.

Perfect for Weddings and Events

Whether creating bridal bouquets, floral arches, or luxury centerpieces, long stem roses help florists achieve elegant and memorable designs.
