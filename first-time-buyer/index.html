FIRST-TIME BUYER QUESTIONS

A Few Questions You May Already Have.

Can I buy Ecuadorian roses if I have never imported flowers before?

Yes. You can start by explaining what you need and asking questions about the sourcing and shipping process. We help you understand the information needed for a first order and explain the steps involved in coordinating delivery from Ecuador to the USA.

What information do I need to request a quote?

Helpful details include the rose variety or style you are looking for, preferred stem length, color, approximate quantity, delivery address, and the date you need the flowers. If you do not know every detail yet, send us what you do know.

What if I do not know which Ecuadorian rose variety to choose?

You can describe your event, floral design, preferred colors, flower form, or overall style. We can then help you understand the available options and the information needed to build your order.

How are Ecuadorian roses shipped to the USA?

After the roses are prepared and packed in Ecuador, international transportation is coordinated through DHL Express. Shipment tracking provides visibility during the journey toward the U.S. delivery address.

Do I have to place an order immediately?

No. You can begin by asking questions and understanding your options. The first conversation is an opportunity to discuss your requirements and determine the next practical step.

Can Farm Direct help me if I am a first-time flower importer?

Yes. The page and inquiry process are designed to help first-time buyers understand the basic product, order, and shipping information needed to source Ecuadorian roses for delivery in the USA.
