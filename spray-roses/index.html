01 The Beauty of More

One stem, multiple moments.

Spray roses are valued for the way several flower heads emerge from a single stem, creating a naturally abundant appearance without losing the delicate character of a rose.

Their versatility makes them especially suited to bouquets, wedding work, floral installations, retail programs, and designs where texture and movement are part of the story.
