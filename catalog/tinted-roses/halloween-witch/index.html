The Halloween Witch Effect

A rose designed to command the room.

Some flowers blend into an arrangement. Halloween Witch was created to do the opposite. Its deep black petals create a dramatic foundation, while irregular white detailing introduces contrast, movement, and a distinctly artistic character.

For floral designers, the result is a rose that can become part of the visual story rather than simply supporting it. It works beautifully when paired with darker foliage, textured materials, candlelight, metallic accents, or unexpected seasonal elements.

DESIGN DIRECTION Dramatic contrast. Unexpected texture. Seasonal personality.
